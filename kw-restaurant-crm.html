Restaurant CRM: turn covers into relationships

28 Sep 2026 · ~5 min read

A restaurant CRM connects guest identity with visit history so you can invite people back with relevant offers—without interrupting service.

What restaurant CRM usually includes

  • Guest profiles linked to visits or orders
  • Tags for preferences, parties, or allergens (where appropriate)
  • Campaign tools for win-back and occasions
  • Reporting on frequency and reactivation

Loyalty and CRM together

Loyalty tracks the earn/burn loop; CRM decides who to message and when. Many restaurant customer loyalty programs sit inside or beside CRM so points and outreach share one guest record.

Operator checklist

  1. Capture consent and contact details at join
  2. Define dormant thresholds (e.g., 30/60/90 days)
  3. Keep floor staff scripts short and consistent
  4. Review offer redemptions against food cost
