CRM software for small business Singapore: start lean

28 Sep 2026 · ~5 min read

Small teams in Singapore need CRM software that captures guests, segments by recency, and supports simple follow-ups—without a full marketing department.

Lean CRM scope

  • Clean contact list with consent
  • Last-visit or last-order fields
  • Basic tags (VIP, dormant, new)
  • Simple message templates for win-back

When to connect loyalty

If you already run stamps or points, connect loyalty earn/burn to the same guest profile. Separate systems that cannot share identity create duplicate work and blind spots.

Buying pitfalls

  1. Buying enterprise automation before a weekly review habit exists
  2. Ignoring counter speed and staff permissions
  3. Counting messages sent instead of visits recovered
  4. Skipping a written data and consent process
